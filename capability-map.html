Capability Map & Sequencing

The sequencing approach prioritizes decision velocity, value realization speed, and implementation risk reduction while preserving governance.

Phase 1 · Stabilize Forecast Core

SSP: Sales Performance Management Activate collaborative forecasting process as primary operating baseline.
SOP: Opportunity Management Tighten stage and category hygiene to improve forecast signal quality.
DPA: Predictive AI / Einstein Scoring Introduce conversion-rate based forecast confidence.

Phase 2 · Operationalize AI Guidance

DAG: Agentic AI / Autonomous Agents Deploy role-oriented inspection and proactive risk prompts.
ARI: Revenue Intelligence Add movement and category-change signals for leadership reviews.
CAF: Agentforce in Slack Push forecast insight into collaboration workflows.

Phase 3 · Expand Decision Fabric

DDH: Data Harmonization Integrate adjacent data sources while keeping forecasting decisions in Salesforce.
IEI: Enterprise Integration Connect ecosystem dependencies without creating governance fragmentation.
PGV: Data Governance & Privacy Scale adoption with clear trust and compliance posture.
