Salesforce Capability Domains

Capabilities are grouped to show both in-scope forecasting enablers and adjacent expansion pathways that preserve a single platform operating model.

Sales Core

SSP, SOP, SLM, and STM form the execution baseline for category-driven and quota-aware forecasting.

Data & AI

DPA, DAG, DDH, DUP, and DEL deliver predictive confidence, agentic guidance, and trust controls.

Analytics & Reporting

ABI, ARI, and APC connect forecast signal visibility to faster leadership action.

Integration & Collaboration

IEI, IEP, and CAF keep adjacent data and team response loops inside the flow of work.

Implication for Consolidation

Blackbaud can prioritize an architecture where forecasting, risk interpretation, and action orchestration converge inside Salesforce rather than dispersing across stand-alone tools that increase operational drag.
